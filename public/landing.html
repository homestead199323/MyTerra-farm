FAQ

Fair questions.

Built by a grower, for beginners

Most farming software is written for people who already farm. MyTerra is made by an independent grower in Albania who practises biointensive gardening and raises his own seedlings, for complete beginners who want the next job in plain words.

I've never grown anything. Is this for me?

Yes, that's exactly who it's for. MyTerra assumes zero knowledge: it tells you what to do, when and why, one small job at a time. If you can follow a game quest, you can follow a carrot bed.

Do I need a farm?

No. A few pots on a balcony count. MyTerra has separate map styles for balconies, backyards and farms, and plans around the space you actually have.

How does MyTerra decide what I should do?

It combines your space, its size and your region with its crop and animal data, your region's planting calendar, the dates you planted things and your local forecast from Open-Meteo. It's guidance, not a guarantee: soil, weather and pests vary, so use your own judgement for big decisions.

Which regions does it cover?

The base data is tuned for Western Europe's maritime climate (UK, Ireland, Benelux, northern France). Regional settings adjust planting windows and varieties for Northern Europe, the Mediterranean, and the warmer and colder parts of the US and Canada. Other climates aren't covered yet.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Once you've opened it online, your jobs, guides, map and logs all work with no connection. Changes sync to your account when you're back in range.

Phone or laptop?

Both. MyTerra runs in any modern browser and installs to your phone's home screen like an app. Your farm syncs across every device you sign in on.

What happens after the 7 days?

You choose Basic, Pro or Lifetime to keep going. No card is taken up front, so nothing charges automatically. If you don't pick a plan, your farm stays saved and viewable (read-only) until you do.

Is my data private?

Your farm is private to your account. We don't sell data or show ads, and you can export everything as a file at any time. Details are in the privacy policy.
